Magenta Arbitrary Image Stylization (TensorFlow Lite)
=====================================================

This directory bundles the official Magenta "arbitrary-image-stylization-v1-256"
int8 TensorFlow Lite models:

  style_predict_int8.tflite
  style_transform_int8.tflite

Source:
  https://tfhub.dev/google/lite-model/magenta/arbitrary-image-stylization-v1-256/int8/prediction/1
  https://tfhub.dev/google/lite-model/magenta/arbitrary-image-stylization-v1-256/int8/transfer/1
  https://github.com/magenta/magenta
